01 / The proposition

Old hardware.
New frontier.

Wildstar64 takes the constraints seriously: low-poly worlds, deliberate engineering, and a driving model built around momentum—not just aim and fire.

Every world is a new battlefield. Every fight has weight.

Inspired by the spirit of physics-led vehicle action, Wildstar64 is an original game and setting. You pilot a powerful machine through hostile terrain, fight tactical enemies, and extract the energy cores that keep each run alive.

One project, two complementary modes: a story-driven campaign in development, and a replayable sector-based Challenge Mode that already provides the core combat loop.

01 / SYSTEMS

Feel the machine

Raycast suspension, track grip, drift, recoil, impacts and self-righting make the Ranger a vehicle with mass and consequence.

02 / WORLDS

Worlds from a seed

Terrain, sites, navigation and object meshes are generated deterministically. Golden-seed checks verify repeatable output.

03 / ENCOUNTERS

Enemies with intent

Grazers flee and raise alarms; Stalkers patrol, investigate, flank and fire. Cores turn combat into an extraction objective.

04 / CAMPAIGN

A machine. A family. A world on the edge.

In-engine story scenes, expressive comms portraits, mission-specific set pieces and an authored narrative expand the procedural action into a character-led adventure. Missions 00–03 are built; the story continues on the roadmap.

05 / CRAFT

Designed for the console

Made with libdragon and Tiny3D, targeting the N64 with an Expansion Pak. Performance, memory and frame pacing are measured—not assumed.

03 / Honest status

A real game.
A real next step.

The current build is a playable development slice—not a finished commercial release. The opportunity is to turn a technically proven foundation into a polished, complete game.

Playable now

M1 / Slice

The sector-based mode demonstrates the core loop on N64: drive, fight, gather cores, extract. The game has a title flow, options, HUD, audio, and several playable campaign sectors.

  • Ranger physics, two enemy types, combat and power cores
  • Procedural sectors with 24 campaign seed outputs checked against golden hashes
  • Three campaign missions built through Mission 03, including Rox's on-foot scavenge and the midpoint battle
  • Host simulation tests, generator inspection tools, and hardware performance measurements

Needs investment

Production phase

Some core risks are retired; production, optimization and completion remain. The next campaign milestone and the full release path require dedicated time and resources.

  • Campaign ends after Mission 03 today; Mission 04 and later acts are not yet built
  • Challenge Mode has not yet been expanded into the planned roguelite run structure
  • Stock N64 graphics and music performance still need focused optimization and hardware validation
  • Remaining missions, content, playtesting, polish and release preparation need funding
No release date or final budget is being represented as confirmed.

04 / Roadmap proposal

From slice to launch.

A production sequence for publisher discussion, grounded in the repo's C3–C5 milestones. Dates and scope should be set jointly after a technical and commercial review.

Foundation / delivered in part

M1 + C0–C2

Playable sector loop, procedural generation, core simulation, and campaign through Mission 03. Continue validating gameplay and real hardware.

Next / C3

Expand the world

Build Mission 04: Night of the Wraiths. Add walkable Scrap-Town locations, on-foot enemies and allies, and the next campaign story beats.

Then / R0 + C4

Complete the journey

Develop Challenge Mode runs, then the remaining campaign missions and finale. Tune performance, content scope and hardware targets with the partner.

Release / C5

Polish & ship

Finish presentation, music, credits, accessibility and playtest revisions; verify on target hardware; prepare the agreed release and marketing package.

Indicative planning only: the design document estimates work by relative effort, not calendar dates. This roadmap is a proposed sequence, not a committed schedule; timeline, budget, deliverables and platform strategy require publisher alignment.

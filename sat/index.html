Everything here is generated at runtime: the Earth's continents, oceans, cities and cloud deck are baked from domain-warped simplex noise into equirectangular render targets; the Milky Way is baked into an HDR cube map; the satellite is built from primitives with canvas-drawn hull and photovoltaic textures; and all audio is synthesised live with WebAudio (no samples). Orbital figures are computed from the scene geometry: altitude from the scaled Earth radius (6371 km), circular velocity v = √(GM/r) and period T = 2πr/v, with the ground track advancing at the true orbital rate plus sidereal rotation at 51.6° inclination. The pale orb is the Moon, lit by a Sun off to the right, so its phase is physically consistent with the lighting on the Earth and spacecraft.
